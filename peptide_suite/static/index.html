EVERY POSITION AGAINST EVERY RESIDUE

The optimize tab ranks the same scan and reports the best five. This is the whole grid, unranked: where nothing helps is a result too, and a top-five list cannot show it. Cells the pipeline could not compute are hatched rather than coloured — the middle of a diverging scale means "no change", and that is a different statement from "no data".

Homologous sequences (optional)

The conservation metric needs three or more distinct homologous sequences. Without them every cell of that metric comes back marked not computed, which is the honest answer and also a good look at what the hatch means.

WHY SEQUENCE-AWARE EVALUATION MATTERS

Peptide datasets are full of near-duplicates — alanine scans, point variants, truncation series. A random split puts a peptide in train and its mutant in test, and the model gets credit for recalling a sequence it has already seen. Held-out families remove that. The gap between the two numbers is the part of the random score that was recall.

Runs live rather than serving a stored table, so the numbers cannot drift from the code. Synthetic by design: the claim is about evaluation protocol, not biology.

REPRESENTATION SPACE

A reference peptide and every single substitution of it, projected onto their first two principal components. The headline is the explained variance, not the scatter: a picture carrying a small share of the variation is a picture in which proximity means very little, and that has to be read before the plot is.

Extra candidates (optional)

Multi-substitution designs or related peptides to place in the same space. One per line. Anything of a different length is marked unrelated rather than given a substitution count it does not have.

DOES FUSION ACTUALLY HELP?

Two modalities — position-aware sequence, which can see a motif, and bulk physicochemistry, which cannot — against a label that needs both. Four arms: each modality alone, naive concatenation, and a gated learned fusion, all on a sequence-clustered split. The answer reported is the verdict rule's, not the winning number: arms whose bootstrap intervals overlap have not been separated by this test, and a comparison that cannot clear a shuffled-label null has not compared anything.

Synthetic by construction, like the split comparison above. It demonstrates that the comparison can be run and read, and supports no biological claim whatever the numbers say.
